Lab Run

2D Mobile Endless Runner

School Project 4 Weeks Unity
🎮 Play on itch.io ▶ Watch Trailer

Overview

Lab Run is a 2D endless runner mobile game developed in Unity during a 4-week school production cycle.

  • 3 Unique Biomes with distinct visuals and obstacles
  • Animal Fusion System combining abilities for strategic variation
  • Procedural Obstacles ensuring dynamic replayability
  • Upgradeable Skill Tree driven by collectible currency

My Contribution

  • Gameplay Prototyping: Implemented core mechanics and validated early gameplay feel
  • Level Design: Designed progression pacing and obstacle composition
  • Lighting & Post-Processing: Enhanced clarity and atmosphere
  • Documentation: Contributed to structured GDD communication
  • FMOD Integration: Assisted with sound implementation
