The Witcher III : Wild Hunt Revenge of Ofir

A Witcher 3 Fanmade Expansion

Team Project 25 Developers RedKit
Work in Progress

Overview

Revenge of Ofir is a large-scale fan expansion set in the eastern empire of Ofir. The project introduces a new explorable region and expanded systems built using RedKit.

My Role

  • Designing and implementing explorable POIs
  • Contributing to the overall open world structure including road networks and POI placement
  • Designing encounters and gameplay sections within the open world
  • Collaborating with quest writers, environment artists and 3D artists
