G.B.I (Ghost Bureau of Investigation)

Two Player CO-OP Puzzle Experience

School Project 7 Weeks Unreal Engine
🎮 Play on itch.io ▶ Watch Trailer

Overview

G.B.I is a 2-player CO-OP Puzzle game developed in Unreal Engine during a 7-week school project.

What's special:

  • Two distinct player classes (Light & Heavy) with unique abilities that require constant cooperation
  • Three unique levels designed around synergy and communication
  • Online multiplayer using Steam's backend

My Role

In this project, I was the Product Owner and Lead Game Designer, primarily responsible for:

  • Defining and owning the overall game vision and design direction
  • Leading cross-team communication between design, art and programming
  • Designing and iterating levels with a strong focus on guidance, composition and readability

Reflection

This game project was a personal challenge for me. It gave me the opportunity to step up, clearly communicate my ideas to the rest of the team, and handle the pressure of delivering. It showed me the importance of saying no and staying true to the design decisions I believed were best for the final product. There were ups and downs, but it proved to be a valuable experience that helped me grow as a game developer.
